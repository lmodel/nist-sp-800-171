# Explanations

* [Scope, influences and mappings](scope-and-influences.md) - What the schema renders and what it leaves out: SP 800-171 Revision 3 with the procedures of SP 800-171A Revision 3 and nothing beyond them, with lmodel/slsa as its one influence on layout. It maps its classes to lmodel/oscal's, as NIST's catalog renders them, carries each requirement's SP 800-53 source controls as data, and names the publications the requirements reference; it models neither the publication's other chapters, SP 800-172 nor the withdrawn requirements' former text.
* [Element names and the shared namespace](element-names.md) - How the schema names its elements so that its adapt copy can sit beside LOKF's schema and the slsa, github-actions, oscal, spdx and nist-sp-800-218 copies: classes with the `Sp800171` prefix, every slot qualified with the publication's own noun, NIST's identifiers kept in the publication's forms, `title` and `description` carrying LOKF's IRIs, and no other key shared, which `tests/test_schema_rules.py` checks.
