# Glossary

* [Family](family.md) - In SP 800-171, one of the seventeen groups the security requirements are sorted into, numbered 03.01 Access Control to 03.17 Supply Chain Risk Management. The schema renders a family as `Sp800171Family`, with its number in `family_number`.
* [Security requirement](security-requirement.md) - In SP 800-171, a numbered, named requirement for protecting CUI, such as 03.01.01 Account Management, with its statement, discussion, references and SP 800-53 source controls, the organization-defined parameters its statement leaves open, and the assessment objectives and methods SP 800-171A gives it. The schema renders one as `Sp800171Requirement`, with its identifier in `requirement_id`.
* [Withdrawn requirement](withdrawn-requirement.md) - In SP 800-171 Revision 3, a requirement of an earlier revision that the revision withdrew: it keeps its number, is marked withdrawn, and names the requirements that incorporate or address what it required where the catalog states them. The schema renders one as an `Sp800171Requirement` with `requirement_status: withdrawn`.
* [Organization-defined parameter (ODP)](organization-defined-parameter.md) - In SP 800-171, a value, such as a time period or a frequency, that a requirement statement leaves to the organization, identified as SP 800-171A writes it, such as A.03.01.01.ODP[01], with the guideline SP 800-171A gives for defining it; a few are selections among choices. The schema renders one as `Sp800171Parameter`, with its identifier in `odp_id`.
* [Assessment objective](assessment-objective.md) - In SP 800-171A, one determination statement that an assessor checks for a requirement, such as A.03.01.01.b[01], tied to the requirement or to one item of its statement. The schema renders one as `Sp800171AssessmentObjective`, with its identifier in `requirement_objective_id`.
* [Assessment method](assessment-method.md) - In SP 800-171A, one of the three methods applied to a requirement, examine, interview or test, with the assessment objects (documents, people, processes or mechanisms) it is applied to. The schema renders one as `Sp800171AssessmentMethod`, with the method in `requirement_method_kind` and the objects in `requirement_method_objects`.
