# System and Information Integrity (03.14)

NIST's catalog renders the family as the group `SP_800_171_03.14`, titled System and Information Integrity, with 8 numbered requirements.[^nist_sp800-171_rev3_catalog.json] Its 5 requirements in force carry 3 organization-defined parameters and 24 assessment objectives:

- `03.14.01` Flaw Remediation
- `03.14.02` Malicious Code Protection
- `03.14.03` Security Alerts, Advisories, and Directives
- `03.14.06` System Monitoring
- `03.14.08` Information Management and Retention

Revision 3 withdrew three of the family's requirements:

- `03.14.04`, incorporated into `03.14.02`
- `03.14.05`, addressed by `03.14.02`
- `03.14.07`, incorporated into `03.14.06`
