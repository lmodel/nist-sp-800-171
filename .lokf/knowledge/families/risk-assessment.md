# Risk Assessment (03.11)

NIST's catalog renders the family as the group `SP_800_171_03.11`, titled Risk Assessment, with 4 numbered requirements.[^nist_sp800-171_rev3_catalog.json] Its 3 requirements in force carry 5 organization-defined parameters and 12 assessment objectives:

- `03.11.01` Risk Assessment
- `03.11.02` Vulnerability Monitoring and Scanning
- `03.11.04` Risk Response

Revision 3 withdrew one of the family's requirements:

- `03.11.03`, incorporated into `03.11.02`
