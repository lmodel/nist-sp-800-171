# nist-sp-800-171 Knowledge Bundle

A [LOKF](https://lokf.nolan-nichols.com) knowledge base for nist-sp-800-171. Every Markdown file under `knowledge/` is one concept; together they form a queryable knowledge graph, derived from this repository's code and docs.

# Services

* [Documentation site](services/documentation-site.md) - The Astro Starlight site in `website/`, for <https://lmodel.github.io/nist-sp-800-171>: the README as its home page, the pages of `docs/`, the schema's element pages, a page per concept of this bundle with its trust label, and the bundle's exports, built and published by Knowledge website.

# Datasets

* [nist-sp-800-171 LinkML schema](datasets/nist-sp-800-171-linkml-schema.md) - The LinkML schema of NIST SP 800-171 Revision 3, `src/nist_sp_800_171/schema/nist_sp_800_171.yaml` (schema id `https://w3id.org/lmodel/nist-sp-800-171`, version 3.0.0): eight classes, three enums and seven identifier types. Its `lokf adapt` copy `.lokf/nist-sp-800-171.yaml`, rooted on the family, the security requirement and the organization-defined parameter, is the bundle's domain schema.
* [NIST's SP 800-171 catalog copy](datasets/sp-800-171-catalog-copy.md) - NIST's catalog of SP 800-171 Revision 3 in the Open Security Controls Assessment Language (OSCAL), which carries the assessment procedures of SP 800-171A Revision 3 too, copied with NIST's licence from usnistgov/oscal-content v1.6.0 into `src/nist_sp_800_171/schema/oscal-content/` and matched to that release by git object hash. The tests derive the whole publication from it.
* [Generated artefacts](datasets/generated-artefacts.md) - What the LinkML generators and `lokf adapt` write from the schema: `project/` (JSON Schema, OWL, SHACL, ShEx, JSON-LD, GraphQL, protobuf, SQL, TypeScript, Excel and Java), the Python dataclasses and Pydantic models in `src/nist_sp_800_171/datamodel/`, `docs/schema/` and `docs/elements/`, and `.lokf/nist-sp-800-171.yaml`. None is edited by hand.
* [Test data and tests](datasets/test-data.md) - The schema's tests: nine valid examples and 43 counter-examples under `tests/data/`, `tests/test_data.py`, which holds each to the validator, `tests/test_upstream.py`, which derives the publication from NIST's catalog, `tests/test_watch.py`, which runs the upstream watch against a stubbed upstream, and `tests/test_schema_rules.py`, which holds the adapt copy apart from LOKF and the slsa, github-actions, oscal, spdx and nist-sp-800-218 copies in `tests/neighbours/`.

# Explanations

* [Scope, influences and mappings](explanations/scope-and-influences.md) - What the schema renders and what it leaves out: SP 800-171 Revision 3 with the procedures of SP 800-171A Revision 3 and nothing beyond them, with lmodel/slsa as its one influence on layout. It maps its classes to lmodel/oscal's, as NIST's catalog renders them, carries each requirement's SP 800-53 source controls as data, and names the publications the requirements reference; it models neither the publication's other chapters, SP 800-172 nor the withdrawn requirements' former text.
* [Element names and the shared namespace](explanations/element-names.md) - How the schema names its elements so that its adapt copy can sit beside LOKF's schema and the slsa, github-actions, oscal, spdx and nist-sp-800-218 copies: classes with the `Sp800171` prefix, every slot qualified with the publication's own noun, NIST's identifiers kept in the publication's forms, `title` and `description` carrying LOKF's IRIs, and no other key shared, which `tests/test_schema_rules.py` checks.

# Glossary

* [Family](glossary/family.md) - In SP 800-171, one of the seventeen groups the security requirements are sorted into, numbered 03.01 Access Control to 03.17 Supply Chain Risk Management. The schema renders a family as `Sp800171Family`, with its number in `family_number`.
* [Security requirement](glossary/security-requirement.md) - In SP 800-171, a numbered, named requirement for protecting CUI, such as 03.01.01 Account Management, with its statement, discussion, references and SP 800-53 source controls, the organization-defined parameters its statement leaves open, and the assessment objectives and methods SP 800-171A gives it. The schema renders one as `Sp800171Requirement`, with its identifier in `requirement_id`.
* [Withdrawn requirement](glossary/withdrawn-requirement.md) - In SP 800-171 Revision 3, a requirement of an earlier revision that the revision withdrew: it keeps its number, is marked withdrawn, and names the requirements that incorporate or address what it required where the catalog states them. The schema renders one as an `Sp800171Requirement` with `requirement_status: withdrawn`.
* [Organization-defined parameter (ODP)](glossary/organization-defined-parameter.md) - In SP 800-171, a value, such as a time period or a frequency, that a requirement statement leaves to the organization, identified as SP 800-171A writes it, such as A.03.01.01.ODP[01], with the guideline SP 800-171A gives for defining it; a few are selections among choices. The schema renders one as `Sp800171Parameter`, with its identifier in `odp_id`.
* [Assessment objective](glossary/assessment-objective.md) - In SP 800-171A, one determination statement that an assessor checks for a requirement, such as A.03.01.01.b[01], tied to the requirement or to one item of its statement. The schema renders one as `Sp800171AssessmentObjective`, with its identifier in `requirement_objective_id`.
* [Assessment method](glossary/assessment-method.md) - In SP 800-171A, one of the three methods applied to a requirement, examine, interview or test, with the assessment objects (documents, people, processes or mechanisms) it is applied to. The schema renders one as `Sp800171AssessmentMethod`, with the method in `requirement_method_kind` and the objects in `requirement_method_objects`.

# Playbooks

* [Regenerate and test after a schema change](playbooks/regenerate-and-test.md) - How to change the schema and check it: `uv sync --group dev`, edit `src/nist_sp_800_171/schema/nist_sp_800_171.yaml`, then `just lint`, `just gen-project`, `just gen-doc`, `just lokf-adapt` and `just test`, and the pre-commit hooks before a pull request.
* [Validate data against the schema](playbooks/validate-data.md) - How to validate SP 800-171 data with `linkml-validate` before loading it into the generated Python models, which check less: the dataclasses check no pattern, and the Pydantic models miss the identifier types' patterns. No check covers the nesting of identifiers, a repeated one, or the shape of a withdrawn requirement.
* [Update the upstream pins](playbooks/update-upstream-pins.md) - What to do when the weekly upstream watch reports that NIST registered the next final revision of SP 800-171, changed or moved its catalog in usnistgov/oscal-content, moved the pinned tag, or that the copy drifted: replace the copy whole from one release, move the pins in `scripts/check_upstream_release.py` and the schema header, and run the tests.
* [Cut a release](playbooks/release.md) - How a release is made: Conventional Commits on `main`, an `## [Unreleased]` changelog section, the `v0.0.0` baseline tag that keeps the first release at 0.1.0, and `semantic-release.yml`, which tags the release and dispatches Knowledge Release to attach `schema-<tag>-nist-sp-800-171.yaml` once `KNOWLEDGE_RELEASE_ENABLED` is true.
* [Knowledge sources map](playbooks/knowledge-sources.md) - The librarian's scrape map: every repository location and external page this bundle derives concepts from, the concepts each yields, how to re-check each on a refresh run, and the paths left out on purpose.

# Policies

* [AI covenant](policies/ai-covenant.md) - The repository's rules for work done with artificial intelligence (AI): you own what you submit, an element under the schema renders SP 800-171 or SP 800-171A in the project's own words and cites it, NIST's catalog copy is never edited, and the scheduled librarian proposes changes only through reviewed pull requests.
* [License and attribution](policies/license-and-attribution.md) - The repository's licences: Apache 2.0 for the schema and the code, NIST's public-domain and CC0 terms for the SP 800-171 catalog copy, NIST's statement that SP 800-171 is not subject to copyright in the United States, MIT for the copier template's scaffolding, and Apache 2.0 for the KTL sidecar and website templates.
* [Security policy](policies/security-policy.md) - How to report a vulnerability (GitHub's private vulnerability reporting), which release receives fixes (the latest), what executes in the repository and what holds each surface, the ruleset on `main`, and the two repository settings that are not yet in force.

# Workflows

* [Build and test (main.yaml)](workflows/main.md) - The Build and test workflow, `.github/workflows/main.yaml`: on a push to main and on every pull request, its test job runs `just test` on Python 3.10 to 3.14, and on 3.13 also `just lint` and `just lokf-adapt-check`, the check that `.lokf/nist-sp-800-171.yaml` is current.
* [Lint & docs (lint-and-docs.yaml)](workflows/lint-and-docs.md) - The Lint & docs workflow, `.github/workflows/lint-and-docs.yaml`: on a push to main, every pull request, a Monday 06:00 UTC schedule and by hand, four jobs run ShellCheck, actionlint, the word budgets of CONTRIBUTING.md (1000) and SECURITY.md (900), and markdownlint, lychee and codespell over the Markdown.
* [Upstream watch (upstream-watch.yaml)](workflows/upstream-watch.md) - The Upstream watch workflow, `.github/workflows/upstream-watch.yaml`: every Monday at 07:00 UTC and by hand, its sp800171-catalog job runs `scripts/check_upstream_release.py` against NIST's revisions and usnistgov/oscal-content, and on a finding opens or updates the issue titled Upstream watch: NIST's SP 800-171 has changed.
* [Semantic release (semantic-release.yml)](workflows/semantic-release.md) - The Semantic release workflow, `.github/workflows/semantic-release.yml`: on a pull request into main, its plan job checks that the commits, the changelog and the title would release and previews the release; on a push to main, its release job in the release Environment tags vX.Y.Z, publishes the GitHub release and dispatches Knowledge Release.
* [Knowledge Registrar (knowledge-registrar.yaml)](workflows/knowledge-registrar.md) - The Knowledge Registrar workflow, `.github/workflows/knowledge-registrar.yaml`: the gate on the knowledge bundle, run on pull requests that touch `.lokf/` or `knowledge_bundle/`, every Monday at 06:00 UTC and by hand; it validates the bundle and its relation targets against the domain schema, runs the conventions script, and checks that each new confirmation by a person is backed by an approval or a signed commit.
* [Knowledge Librarian (knowledge-librarian.yaml)](workflows/knowledge-librarian.md) - The Knowledge Librarian workflow, `.github/workflows/knowledge-librarian.yaml`: every Monday at 05:00 UTC and by hand, once `KNOWLEDGE_LIBRARIAN_ENABLED` is true, it runs an agent with the ktl-librarian skill (pinned by `TRUST_LADDER_SKILLS_REF` at v0.59.0) in a read-only job and opens a review pull request from a separate job that runs no agent.
* [Knowledge Release (knowledge-release.yaml)](workflows/knowledge-release.md) - The Knowledge Release workflow, `.github/workflows/knowledge-release.yaml`: dispatched with a release's tag, or started by a published release once `KNOWLEDGE_RELEASE_ENABLED` is true, it attaches the bundle zip, the domain schema as `schema-<tag>-nist-sp-800-171.yaml` with its checksum, and the Copilot skills to the release, when the bundle or the schema changed or `force` is set.
* [Knowledge sync (knowledge-sync.yaml)](workflows/knowledge-sync.md) - The Knowledge sync workflow, `.github/workflows/knowledge-sync.yaml`: every Monday at 04:10 UTC and by hand, it moves the sidecar's copies and the skills pin to the newest Knowledge Trust Ladder release through one pull request, opened with the `KNOWLEDGE_SYNC_TOKEN` secret, and holds the workflow changes for a person to approve.
* [Knowledge website (knowledge-website.yaml)](workflows/knowledge-website.md) - The Knowledge website workflow, `.github/workflows/knowledge-website.yaml`: on pushes and pull requests that touch the site's sources, every Monday at 05:23 UTC and by hand, its build job builds `website/` and checks its links read-only, and its deploy job publishes the default branch's build to GitHub Pages once `KNOWLEDGE_WEBSITE_ENABLED` is true.

# References
