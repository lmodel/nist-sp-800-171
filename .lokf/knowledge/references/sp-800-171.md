# NIST SP 800-171 Revision 3

NIST Special Publication (SP) 800-171 Revision 3 is titled "Protecting Controlled Unclassified Information in Nonfederal Systems and Organizations". NIST's page for it gives the date published as May 2024, the authors as Ron Ross and Victoria Pillitteri, and says that it supersedes SP 800-171 Revision 2 of 28 January 2021. Its document history lists drafts on 19 July 2022, 10 May 2023 and 9 November 2023, and the final on 14 May 2024, and a planning note of 25 June 2024 says that an HTML version is available.[^final] The publication is at <https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-171r3>.

NIST's project page for protecting controlled unclassified information (CUI) says that the suite of guidance, SP 800-171, SP 800-171A, SP 800-172 and SP 800-172A, focuses on protecting the confidentiality of CUI and recommends specific security requirements to achieve that objective. The page was created on 13 June 2019 and updated on 16 September 2026.[^protecting-controlled-unclassified-information] [NIST SP 800-171A Revision 3](sp-800-171a.md) gives the assessment procedures for the requirements.

The README says that Revision 3 is the current edition and that NIST has published no draft of a Revision 4.[^readme.md] `scripts/check_upstream_release.py` asks doi.org every week whether NIST has registered `10.6028/NIST.SP.800-171r4`, the identifier the next final revision would take, and reports the draft identifiers it finds for information only.[^check_upstream_release.py] [NIST's SP 800-171 catalog copy](../datasets/sp-800-171-catalog-copy.md) is the machine-readable form of this revision that the repository keeps.
