# NIST SP 800-171A Revision 3

NIST Special Publication (SP) 800-171A Revision 3 is titled "Assessing Security Requirements for Controlled Unclassified Information". NIST's page for it gives the date published as May 2024 and the authors as Ron Ross and Victoria Pillitteri, and says that it supersedes SP 800-171A of 13 June 2018. Its document history lists a draft on 9 November 2023 and the final on 14 May 2024, and a planning note of 25 June 2024 says that an HTML version is available. Its abstract says that it provides assessment procedures and a methodology for assessing the security requirements of SP 800-171, and that the procedures are flexible and can be customized to the needs of organizations and assessors.[^a-r3-final] The publication is at <https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-171Ar3>.

The schema's header names the publication in `see_also`, and the classes `Sp800171Parameter`, `Sp800171AssessmentObjective` and `Sp800171AssessmentMethod` render the guideline it gives each organization-defined parameter, its determination statements and its examine, interview and test methods, which NIST's catalog carries inside each requirement.[^nist_sp_800_171.yaml] [The glossary](../glossary/index.md) defines the three.
