# References

* [NIST SP 800-171 Revision 3](sp-800-171.md) - NIST Special Publication 800-171 Revision 3, Protecting Controlled Unclassified Information in Nonfederal Systems and Organizations (May 2024, <https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-171r3>), the final revision the schema renders. It supersedes Revision 2 of January 2021, and NIST has published no draft of a Revision 4.
* [NIST SP 800-171A Revision 3](sp-800-171a.md) - NIST Special Publication 800-171A Revision 3, Assessing Security Requirements for Controlled Unclassified Information (May 2024, <https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-171Ar3>): the assessment objectives and methods for each requirement of SP 800-171 Revision 3, which NIST's catalog carries and the schema renders.
